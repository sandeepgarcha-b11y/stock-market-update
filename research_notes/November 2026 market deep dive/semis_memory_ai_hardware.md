## 1. Memory & storage: Sandisk, Micron, WDC, Seagate, SK Hynix, Samsung, Kioxia. Is there a supercycle?

### Takeaway
Memory fundamentals are at all-time extremes, with no historical precedent. Over Sep–Oct, gross margins reached about 85–87% at Micron and Sandisk and operating margins about 80% at SK Hynix. Samsung made the first quarterly operating profit above KRW100T. Every company guided the next quarter higher still. Even so, the shares have crashed 30–65% from their highs. Investors are pricing a cycle peak for three reasons: NAND contract prices went flat in October (the first sign of a plateau), Kioxia's December-quarter guidance missed, and post-midterm fears about AI regulation and capex hit the whole group. Memory stocks now trade at about 3–6x forward earnings. That multiple tells you the market expects the earnings to collapse; it is not evidence that the stocks are cheap.

### Inferences
- Reconciling Micron prices: $855.10 (3 Nov) × 0.93 ≈ $795 (4 Nov), and × 0.825 ≈ $656 (5 Nov). The Fool's $656.39 is therefore probably the 5 Nov close. That puts MU about 48% below its $1,254.61 high and at roughly 4x forward earnings. Its YTD gain has probably shrunk from about +225% (29 Oct) to roughly +130%. This is my calculation and should be checked against a live quote.
- Sandisk: $806 against a peak market value of about $347B (StockAnalysis's ~$115B current value implies ~142M shares) means the stock is roughly 65–67% off its June high. Annualising the FQ2 EPS guidance midpoint (~$56 × 4 ≈ $224) gives a forward P/E of about 3.6x at $806. The Q3 buyback averaged about $1,560 per share (5.0B/3.2M), nearly double the current price. The quoted "+575% YTD" figures are stale. My rough estimate of the true YTD gain is about +200–250%, but I could not find a start-of-year price, so this is unverified.
- WDC at $269.94 on an annualised FQ2 EPS guide of ~$23 trades at about 12x forward. That is cheaper than its June peak but dearer than NAND and DRAM names because HDD is seen as less cyclical. STX: price unavailable after 5 Nov. A drop of about 16% from a level already about 20% off its $994.79 high implies roughly 35–40% below the high (rough).
- Leading indicators are turning before reported earnings: NAND is flat in October, Kioxia guides lower and Q4 hikes are "narrowing". ### Inferences
- Capex guidance is not the issue yet; no hyperscaler cut. The risks are (a) the financing chain (neoclouds, Oracle, SPVs), (b) policy-driven delays to 2027–28 data-centre builds and (c) memory-price normalisation. Signs that would mark the start of a bust: hyperscaler 2027 capex guidance below $1T at the Jan–Feb Q4 results, Nvidia guidance disappointing on 17 Nov, a further drop in NAND or DRAM contract prices in November or December, or widening neocloud credit spreads. Signs that the dip is a buying opportunity: a strong Nvidia result, 2027 guides at or above $1.1T, and stable Q1 2027 memory contract prices.

## 7. UK access (Stocks & Shares ISA)

### Takeaway
All the US-listed names (NVDA, AMD, AVGO, MRVL, INTC, MU, SNDK, WDC, STX, AMAT, LRCX, KLAC, ANET, COHR, LITE, CRDO, ALAB, VRT, ETN, GEV, CLS, CRWV, NBIS, ORCL), plus TSMC (TSM) and ASML ADRs and listings, are normally available in UK ISAs.
