Apple (+25–27% YTD) is the only member clearly beating the index. Meta, Tesla and (by some counts) Alphabet are down YTD; Microsoft and Amazon lag the Nasdaq-100. ## 3. Software: AI disruption of SaaS; Palantir, AppLovin, Snowflake, Datadog, CrowdStrike

### Takeaway
2026 has been the "SaaSpocalypse". Jefferies traders coined the term for the software sell-off that began late Jan/early Feb 2026, triggered by Anthropic's Claude Cowork and enterprise plugins and OpenAI's agent platform. It erased an estimated $1–2T of software market cap on fears that AI agents will erode per-seat pricing. Even the "AI winners" (Palantir, CrowdStrike, Datadog) sold off in early November on beats with cautious guidance.

### Cited Findings
- The selloff began in early Feb 2026, tied to Anthropic's Claude Cowork launch, with about $1T erased — [Bull Oak](https://bulloak.com/blog/the-2026-saaspocalypse-what-the-ai-software-selloff-means-for-your-portfolio/). Another tracker dates it from Anthropic enterprise plugins on 27 Jan and OpenAI's agent platform on 5 Feb, with more than $1T lost — [Serenities AI](https://serenitiesai.com/articles/1-trillion-saas-selloff-ai-replacing-software-2026). A third puts about $2T lost over 12 months — [Tech Insider](https://tech-insider.org/saas-stock-crash-ai-agents-2-trillion-2026/). (Blog-quality sources; magnitudes conflict.)
- By 22 Jan 2026, Salesforce, ServiceNow and Adobe were already down more than 14% and the sell-off was setting up M&A — [CNBC, 22 Jan 2026](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/01/22/selloff-in-software-from-ai-sets-stage-for-potential-big-year-of-ma.html).
