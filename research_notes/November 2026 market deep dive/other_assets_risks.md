## Precious metals: gold, silver, platinum, central-bank buying, outlook

### Takeaway
Gold set an all-time high of about $5,590–5,627 on 28–29 Jan 2026. It then went through a "January crash", rallied in August to about $4,634–4,638, and slid to a one-year low of about $3,875–3,900 on 30 Oct. The main driver was oil-fed inflation pushing the Fed (and the BoE) toward rate **hikes**, plus forced liquidation. Central-bank buying is strong (321t in Q3), so the slide is about rates and positioning, not lost structural demand. That is the contrarian case.
