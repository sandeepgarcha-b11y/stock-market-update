## 1. UK: FTSE 100/250 performance, winners/losers, Budget impact, M&A/IPOs, this week's updates

### Takeaway
The FTSE 100 is up about 8–9% YTD (around 10,750–10,800 on 5–6 Nov). That is below its 27 Feb record of 10,910.55 but it has been relatively resilient in the global tech rout, because the index is heavy in energy, miners, pharma, tobacco and banks. Big UK stories: (a) the 28 Oct Budget lifted banks (the surcharge was smaller than feared) and crushed housebuilders; (b) the BoE *raised* rates to 4% on 5 Nov; (c) defence names are falling on Iran/Ukraine peace hopes and the US midterm result; (d) HSBC slumped in October after a Q3 revenue miss; (e) the IPO market is thin apart from Airtel Money.

### Inferences
- The FTSE 100's tilt towards old-economy sectors (oil majors, miners, tobacco, pharma, Imperial/BAT) is protecting it in an AI/tech rout, much as it did in 2022. A UK investor's home index is the "defensive" leg right now.

- Mid-cap M&A (Mobico, Ashtead Technology, Eleco, Harworth) continues the 2024–25 pattern of discounted UK small and mid caps being taken private.
