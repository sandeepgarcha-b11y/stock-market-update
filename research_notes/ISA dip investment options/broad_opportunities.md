## Q1. Regional valuations (forward P/E, CAPE, dividend yield), 2026 YTD returns, and distance from highs

On every measure found, the US remains the most expensive major region: about 19–20x forward earnings and a CAPE of about 36. ## Q4. Non-equity options inside an S&S ISA: gilts and bonds, money market or cash-like ETFs, gold

### Takeaway
UK gilt yields are at multi-decade highs: the 10-year hit a 19-year high of 5.527% in early October and was about 5.25–5.32% after the 28 Oct Budget. That makes gilts and gilt ETFs a genuinely attractive defensive sleeve, and JPM's 2027 LTCMA puts global aggregate bonds at 5.3%, its highest in two decades. The risk is that the BoE (Bank Rate 3.75%, CPI 3.1%) is expected to start hiking, possibly from 5 Nov.

Sterling money market ETFs yield about 3.6–3.75% net, a reasonable "dry powder" home while phasing in. Gold is about 20% below its January 2026 record (about $4,070–4,160 in October), which makes it a potential diversifier on a dip.

Individual low-coupon gilts are already CGT-free outside an ISA, so ISA space is better used for gilt funds or ETFs, high-coupon gilts, or equities.
