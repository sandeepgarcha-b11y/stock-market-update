## 1. Which UCITS ETFs available in the UK cover robotics, automation, AI, semiconductors and humanoids?

### Takeaway
There is a good range of ISA-eligible, Irish- or Luxembourg-domiciled UCITS ETFs. Here is what each one gives you:
- **Broad robotics/automation:** iShares RBOT/RBTX (TER 0.40%, about $4bn AUM, the strongest recent performer) and L&G ROBO/ROBG (0.80%, about €1.6bn, close to equal-weighted).
- **Robotics + AI with heavy concentration:** Global X BOTZ (0.50%, small at about $143m).
- **AI software and platforms:** Xtrackers XAIX (0.35%, about €7.5–8.4bn, mega-cap heavy), WisdomTree WTAI/INTL (0.40%) and L&G AIAG (0.49%).
- **Semiconductors:** VanEck SMH/SMGB (0.35%, about $8.3bn, 25 US-listed names) and iShares SEMI (0.35%, about $5.7bn, global).
- **Humanoid robotics:** KraneShares KOID UCITS, listed on the LSE on 9 Oct 2025 (0.69%).

The cheapest large funds are XAIX, SMGB and SEMI at 0.35%, then RBOT/RBTX at 0.40%. ROBO is expensive at 0.80%.

### Inferences
- **Overlap.**
  - **Nvidia** appears in nearly every fund. It is a top-5 holding in BOTZ (about 8%), a top-5 in XAIX (about 4.8%), a core holding in both semiconductor ETFs, and about 5% of a global tracker (see Section 3).
  - **AMD** is a top holding in RBOT/RBTX, AIAG and WTAI.
  - **XAIX** has a top 10 of Microsoft, Meta, Apple and Nvidia. That overlaps heavily with what a global tracker already holds, so it adds little beyond a tilt to the mega-caps.
  - **ROBO/ROBG** (near equal-weight, top 10 about 20%) and **AIAG/WTAI** (top holdings 2–3% each) spread across more names. They add the most mid-cap exposure that a global tracker doesn't already have.
  - **BOTZ** is the most concentrated: Intuitive Surgical, Fanuc, Keyence, Nvidia and ABB make up about 42% of the fund.
- **Best purer robotics/automation pick.** RBOT/RBTX combines low cost (0.40%), size (about $4bn) and the strongest recent performance. Its mid-2026 data shows heavy semiconductor exposure (Intel, AMD, Advantest), so it overlaps with SMGB/SEMI.
- **Purest "physical AI" choice.** BOTZ, given Intuitive Surgical, Fanuc and Keyence plus Nvidia. It is small (about $143m) and has lagged.
- **Humanoid robotics.** KOID is the only verified UCITS option. It is under 13 months old and costs 0.69%. Treat it as a small satellite holding at most.
- **Semiconductors.** SMGB holds 25 US-listed names, capped at 10% each, so non-US firms appear only through US ADRs such as TSMC and ASML. SEMI is global, takes in small caps and applies an ESG screen. Both cost 0.35%.

## 2. Active funds and investment trusts with a technology, robotics or AI focus available in ISAs

### Takeaway
Polar Capital Technology Trust (PCT) is the standout investment trust. Its OCF is 0.69% with no performance fee since May 2025, its NAV rose 102% in the year to April 2026, and it trades at about an 8–10% discount. Allianz Technology Trust (ATT) charges about 0.80% at a similar discount. The open-ended robotics funds are dearer: Pictet Robotics at 1.09% and AXA WF Robotech at 0.76–1.00% depending on share class. I could not verify recent performance for either.

### Inferences
- **Investment trusts trade like shares.** They can be bought on Trading 212, Freetrade, AJ Bell, HL and ii with share-dealing charges rather than fund charges. Buying at a discount gives about £1.09 of NAV per £1 at a 9% discount, but the discount can widen.
- **Platform fees on funds.** Pictet and AXA Robotech are open-ended funds. On HL, Fidelity and AJ Bell they attract percentage platform fees; HL's is uncapped for funds. Together with OCFs of 0.76–1.09%, they cost 2–3 times as much as RBOT/RBTX.
- **PCT and ATT are broad technology trusts.** They hold mega-cap tech, so they overlap heavily with a global tracker and XAIX. They are not pure robotics plays.

### Gaps
- No verified 1y, 3y or 5y performance for ATT, Pictet Robotics or AXA Robotech in 2026.
- No current top holdings for PCT or ATT.

## 4. Rules: PRIIPs/CCI, US-domiciled ETFs, and buying US and other foreign shares in an ISA

### Takeaway
The UK's Consumer Composite Investments (CCI) regime replaces UK PRIIPs and UCITS KIIDs. It started on 6 April 2026 with an optional transition, and becomes mandatory on 8 June 2027. Despite this, UK retail investors still cannot generally buy US-domiciled ETFs (US BOTZ, ROBO, HUMN, BOTT, SMH) in practice. US issuers don't produce UK disclosure documents, and platforms keep the funds blocked. Individual US, European and (on some platforms) Japanese or Hong Kong shares can be held in an ISA. For US shares, file a W-8BEN; US dividends then suffer 15% withholding, which cannot be reclaimed in an ISA.

### Cited Findings
- **CCI timetable.** The legislation took effect on 6 Apr 2026 and starts an 18-month optional transition. The regime is mandatory from 8 Jun 2027. It replaces PRIIPs and UCITS disclosure (the KID and KIID), and it covers overseas firms and unauthorised persons too — [Mondaq](https://www.mondaq.com/uk/financial-services/1721448/new-fca-retail-disclosure-regime); [A&O Shearman](https://finreg.aoshearman.com/uk-fca-policy-statement-and-final-rules-on-new-consumer-composite-investment-regime); [Hogan Lovells](https://hlc.com/en/publications/uk-fca-publishes-final-rules-on-consumer-composite-investments-regime).
- **US ETFs remain blocked.** Platforms show US funds as untradable because they lack UK-format disclosure — [CJ Finance](https://www.cjfinance.co.uk/investing-us-citizen-uk/).
  - One commentator argues CCI lowers the barrier but doesn't open US ETFs by itself. VOO and VTI are expected to stay "professional only" unless the UK grants the US equivalence — [Edale](https://edale.co/us-uk-etf-kid-cci-reporting/).
  - The usual route is a UCITS ETF tracking the same index — [IG UK](https://www.ig.com/uk/trading-strategies/how-to-buy-us-etfs-in-the-uk--investing-and-trading-220620); [investingintheweb](https://investingintheweb.com/blog/vti-europe-uk-alternatives/).
- **W-8BEN and US dividends.** The W-8BEN cuts US dividend withholding from 30% to 15%. Brokers need it before you can deal in US shares — [Fidelity UK](https://www.fidelity.co.uk/international-shares/w-8ben-form/).
  - Inside an ISA the 15% still applies and cannot be reclaimed, because there is no UK tax to credit it against — [calchub](https://calchub.uk/blog/withholding-tax-us-shares-isa-w8ben-2026-27/); [Boring Money](https://www.boringmoney.co.uk/learn/investing-guides/us-tax-guide-for-uk-investors-expats-understanding-w8ben-forms-isas-and-international-tax-obligations/).
  - Irish UCITS ETFs typically suffer withholding inside the fund instead — [quantroutine](https://quantroutine.com/learn/us-dividend-withholding-tax-non-us/).
- **Foreign markets by platform.**
  - **AJ Bell:** 24 markets including the US, Japan and Europe — [Forbes Advisor UK](https://www.forbes.com/advisor/uk/investing/best-trading-platform-for-beginners/).
  - **ii:** 16 exchanges including Hong Kong, Singapore and Australia. Other international shares cost £9.99 on Core — [StockBrokers.com ii review](https://www.stockbrokers.com/uk/review/interactive-investor); [investplatforms](https://investplatforms.co.uk/blog/hargreaves-lansdown-vs-aj-bell-vs-interactive-investor-which-platform-is-best/).
  - **HL:** the US, Canada and 15 European exchanges, but reportedly no Asia-Pacific — [investplatforms](https://investplatforms.co.uk/blog/hargreaves-lansdown-vs-aj-bell-vs-interactive-investor-which-platform-is-best/).

### Inferences
- **To own a humanoid ETF in an ISA,** use KOID UCITS rather than HUMN, BOTT or the US KOID.
- **To own Fanuc, Keyence or Yaskawa directly,** use AJ Bell (Japan) or ii. HL reportedly lacks Asia-Pacific. Otherwise use a UCITS robotics ETF, which holds them indirectly.
- **US single stocks** (Nvidia, Intuitive Surgical, Tesla and so on) work on every major platform after a W-8BEN. The FX fee is the main cost difference (Section 6).

### Gaps
- I could not open FCA PS25/20 directly, so the exact interaction with the Overseas Funds Regime / US equivalence is unverified.
- No official HL, ii or Trading 212 statement on US ETF access under CCI was found.
- Japanese and Hong Kong share access on Trading 212 and Freetrade was not verified.

## 5. ISA rules for 2026/27, including the Budget of 28 October 2026

### Takeaway
- The 2026/27 allowance is £20,000. The tax year ends 5 April 2027.
- **The Autumn Budget on 28 Oct 2026 reversed the planned April 2027 changes from Budget 2025:**
  - the cash ISA cut to £12k for under-65s
  - the 22% charge on interest from cash held in stocks and shares ISAs
  - the ban on transferring from stocks and shares to cash ISAs
- The cash ISA limit stays at £20k for everyone.
- A new "starter investment ISA" for first-time investors is going to consultation.
- Since April 2024 you can pay into more than one stocks and shares ISA in the same tax year, as long as the total stays within £20k.

### Cited Findings
- **Budget 28 Oct 2026, cash ISA.** The cash ISA limit stays at £20,000 for under-65s, and the £12k cut is cancelled — [MSE](https://www.moneysavingexpert.com/news/2026/10/cash-isa-limit-cut-cancelled/); [Investment Week](https://www.investmentweek.co.uk/news/4537982/budget-26-20k-cash-isa-annual-allowance-reinstated-starter-product-introduced); [Portfolio Adviser](https://portfolio-adviser.com/autumn-budget-2026-healey-u-turns-on-cash-isa-limit/); [UK Parliament Treasury Committee](https://www.parliament.uk/external/committees/news/2026/october/tsc_a-victory-for-common-sense-cash-isa-budget-announcement-welcomed-by-meg-hillier/).
- **Budget 28 Oct 2026, the other planned changes.**
  - The tax on cash interest in stocks and shares ISAs is cancelled — [Which?](https://www.which.co.uk/news/article/stocks-and-shares-isa-cash-interest-tax-cancelled-in-autumn-budget-2026-aebwl6S8X9u6).
  - The transfer restriction is dropped, so transfers continue in both directions — [Tembo](https://www.tembomoney.com/learn/changes-to-isa-savings-you-need-to-know).
  - The wider anti-avoidance measures are not happening — [MSE Martin Lewis analysis](https://www.moneysavingexpert.com/news/2026/10/martin-lewis-budget-2026/).
- **Starter ISA.** The government will work with industry on an incentive for first-time stocks and shares ISA investors — [International Adviser](https://www.international-adviser.com/budget-2026-chancellor-reveals-new-starter-isa/); [MSE](https://www.moneysavingexpert.com/news/2026/10/martin-lewis-budget-2026/).
- **Lifetime ISA.** A replacement is due in April 2028. The penalty for buying a home over £450k falls from 25% to 20% from 29 Oct 2026 — [Which?](https://www.which.co.uk/news/article/chancellor-announces-cash-isa-u-turn-but-hikes-savings-tax-further-aFhLB8n24OfT); [The Private Office](https://www.theprivateoffice.com/insights/budget-2026-key-changes-tax-pensions-and-isas).
- **Savings tax outside ISAs.** Which? reports a further 2 percentage point rise. NatWest describes a "4% rise" from 6 Apr 2027. The difference may be cumulative versus incremental — [Which?](https://www.which.co.uk/news/article/chancellor-announces-cash-isa-u-turn-but-hikes-savings-tax-further-aFhLB8n24OfT); [NatWest](https://www.natwest.com/premier-banking/insights/money-matters/post-budget-update.html).
- **Pre-Budget plan (now superseded).** The Budget 2025 package was announced, with draft regulations published 16 July (2026):
  - cash ISA £12k for under-65s
  - 22% charge
  - transfer ban
  - overall limit kept at £20k until at least April 2031

  Sources: [MSE Jun 2026](https://www.moneysavingexpert.com/news/2026/06/cash-investment-isa-reforms/); [Crowe](https://www.crowe.com/uk/crowefinancialplanning/insights/isa-changes); [Vanguard](https://www.vanguardinvestor.co.uk/articles/latest-thoughts/how-it-works/what-do-the-new-isa-rules-mean-for-investors).
- **Allowance and multiple ISAs.** The 2026/27 allowance is £20,000. Since April 2024 you can open and pay into several stocks and shares ISAs in the same tax year within the £20k total — [rationalgb](https://rationalgb.com/isa-allowance-2026/); [uktax.tools](https://uktax.tools/isa-allowance/).

### Inferences
- **Scale of a £14k contribution.** It uses 70% of the 2026/27 allowance. £6k of headroom remains until 5 April 2027, and the allowance is lost if unused.
- **Splitting across ISAs.** Because several stocks and shares ISAs are allowed in one year, the investor could, for example, hold a global tracker on InvestEngine and US or Japanese single shares on Trading 212 or AJ Bell.
- **Cash in the ISA.** Uninvested cash in a stocks and shares ISA will not face the 22% charge after all, so phasing money in from cash inside the ISA stays tax-neutral.

## 7. Currency: USD and JPY assets, GBP-quoted lines and hedged share classes

### Takeaway
Every thematic ETF found here is unhedged against GBP. No GBP-hedged share class was found for any robotics, AI or semiconductor UCITS ETF. A GBP-quoted line (ROBG, RBTX, SMGB, INTL) only changes the currency the price is shown and traded in. That avoids the platform's FX fee, but the investor still carries full USD, JPY, TWD and EUR currency risk from the underlying holdings.

### Cited Findings
- **Amundi MSCI Robotics & AI:** "Currency hedged · No" — [justETF](https://www.justetf.com/en/etf-profile.html?isin=LU1861132840).
- **WisdomTree AI UCITS:** only the USD Acc line was found, with no GBP-hedged class — [WisdomTree EU](https://www.wisdomtree.eu/en-gb/etfs/thematic/wtai---wisdomtree-artificial-intelligence-ucits-etf---usd-acc).
- **Fund currencies.**
  - L&G ROBO: base currency USD — [extraETF](https://extraetf.com/ch/etf-profile/IE00BMW3QX54).
  - iShares Automation & Robotics: USD Acc share class; USA 54.10%, Japan 16.65% — [Parqet](https://app.parqet.com/etf/ishares-automation-robotics-ucits-etf-usd-acc-IE00BYZK4552).
  - iShares SEMI: USD Acc — [BlackRock](https://www.blackrock.com/lu/individual/en/products/319084/ishares-msci-global-semiconductors-ucits-etf).
- **No FX fee on GBP-priced ETFs** — [investplatforms](https://investplatforms.co.uk/blog/freetrade-trading212-investengine/).
- **FX fees** when buying non-GBP lines or overseas shares: HL 1%, AJ Bell 0.75%, Freetrade Basic 0.99%, Trading 212 0.15% — see Section 6 sources.

### Inferences
- **Why currency matters.** Robotics ETFs are about 54% US and 17% Japan (RBOT/RBTX). Semiconductor ETFs are almost entirely USD-listed or US-centred. A stronger pound lowers GBP returns; a weaker pound raises them.
- **Always pick the GBP line where one exists** (for example SMGB over SMH, ROBG over ROBO, and the GBP iShares line). This avoids FX fees and keeps prices in pounds. It does not hedge.
- **The only route to hedging** for this theme would be a currency-hedged share class, and none was found. Hedging also costs money and removes diversification. For a long-term ISA, unhedged is the norm.

### Gaps
- I did not confirm whether any GBP-hedged class of XAIX, SEMI, SMGB or RBOT exists. None appeared in the searches.
- The exact GBP and USD ticker for each fund was not confirmed against LSE listings.
